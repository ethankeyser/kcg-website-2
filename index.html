Software written for your workflow, not licensed to it.

We develop and maintain our own applications. That means a room-control interface can be shaped around how your staff actually work, and video can be integrated directly into the case-management and documentation systems already in use.

Room Control System

Windows-based control of AV equipment over RS-232 or Telnet. Configurable without writing code, with an enterprise monitoring dashboard across every room.

Roughly one-third less cost than competing systems, using standards-based hardware.

Hardened enclosures, fabricated in-house.

When an installation has to survive a public corridor, a secure facility, or a housing unit, off-the-shelf mounts do not hold up. We fabricate custom steel enclosures to spec — sized to the device, serviceable from the front, and built to be attacked.

  • Securely houses Poly X50, X30, or Logitech Rally Bar Huddle
  • 24″ monitor integrated behind the faceplate
  • Inmate-grade handset, 1,000 lb pull strength, USB to the video device
  • Front case folds down for full access and service
  • Small wall unit dimensions: 24 × 24 × 6″
  • Custom sizes and configurations fabricated to your spec

Why it matters

Most integrators outsource fabrication. We don't — which means the enclosure, the device inside it, and the software driving it are all on the same schedule and the same contract.

How we work

Single-source accountability, start to finish.

Every phase below sits under one contract with one team. There is no point at which a project becomes somebody else's problem.

  1. 01

    Design

    Requirements, sightlines, acoustics, and network considerations resolved before anything is ordered. Vendor-agnostic recommendations driven by the room, not inventory.

  2. 02

    Engineer

    Drawings, bills of material, custom software scoping, and enclosure fabrication drawings produced as one coordinated package.

  3. 03

    Install

    Quality installations by experienced technicians who have worked in secure and occupied facilities before.

  4. 04

    Commission

    Systems tested against the design, integrated with your platforms, and handed over with staff who know how to run them.

  5. 05

    Support

    Preventive maintenance and responsive technical support for the life of the system.
